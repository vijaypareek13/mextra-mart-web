Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Mextra Mart is a grocery delivery shop. This policy explains what the app and the website collect, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. It covers the Mextra Mart Android app, the iOS app and the website.

What we collect

WhatWhy we need it
Your name, email address and mobile number To create your account, sign you in, and contact you about an order.
Your delivery address, including landmark and pincode To bring the order to your door. It is printed on the packing slip.
Your basket and your order history To take the order, show it to you later, and handle returns or questions.

We do not collect your location in the background, your contacts, your photos, or anything on your device outside the app. We do not show advertising and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Payment

Orders are paid in cash on delivery. We do not ask for, handle or store card details, UPI identifiers or bank information. If online payment is added later, it will be handled by a licensed payment provider and this policy will be updated before it goes live.

Who can see your information

  • You — your own account, addresses and orders, after signing in.
  • The shop — staff who pack and deliver your order see your name, phone number, address and what you ordered.
  • Supabase, our database and hosting provider, stores the data on our behalf on servers in Mumbai, India.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else for marketing. We disclose it only where the law requires it.

How it is protected

Everything travels over an encrypted connection. The database enforces, row by row, that a signed-in customer can read only their own profile, addresses, basket and orders. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and nobody at the shop can read them.

How long we keep it

Account details are kept while the account exists. Orders are kept as long as the business records require. When an account is deleted, the account and its addresses are removed; past orders are kept as a sales record with the delivery details they were placed with.

Your choices

  • See and change your name, phone number and addresses in the app under Profile.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account, by writing to us.
  • Delete the app at any time; that stops any further collection from your device.

Children

Mextra Mart is meant for adults placing household orders. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material change will be announced in the app before it takes effect.
