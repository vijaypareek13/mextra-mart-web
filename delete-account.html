Delete your Mextra Mart account

For the Mextra Mart app and website. Last updated 4 October 2026.

You can ask us to delete your Mextra Mart account and the personal information held with it. There is no charge, and you do not have to give a reason.

How to ask

  1. Open the account deletion request form.
  2. Enter the email address you use to sign in to Mextra Mart, so we know the request is yours.
  3. For the app name, write Mextra Mart.
  4. A reason is optional. Submit the form.

Request account deletion

If you would rather write to us, email martmextra@gmail.com with Delete my account as the subject, from the address you sign in with.

We reply within 7 days to confirm, and the account is deleted within 30 days of that.

You can also ask us to delete only part of your information — an old delivery address, for example — and keep your account. Say so in the reason box, or in the email. Your name, phone number and saved addresses can also be changed by yourself at any time, in the app under Profile.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DeletedKept, and why
  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Every saved delivery address
  • Anything left in your basket
  • Orders you have already placed, with the delivery name, address and phone they were placed with. These are the shop's sales records and we are required to keep them for 8 years under Indian tax law.

They are kept as records of a sale, are not linked to a usable account any more, and are never used to contact you or for marketing.

After the 8 years, order records are deleted too.

If you only want to stop using the app

Deleting the app from your phone stops any further collection from your device, but it does not delete your account. Email us as above if you want the account gone.
